# Buying in Melbourne: why taking our time makes sense

**In short:** keep looking and be ready, but buy only when the right house comes up at a fair price. Don't buy just because a house is available.

## 1. Melbourne prices are falling

- Melbourne house prices are down **7.3% over the past year**, and **3.9% in the last three months**.
- Homes are taking longer to sell: **39 days on average**, up from 23 a year ago (all capital cities).
- More homes are for sale than a year ago, and fewer auctions are selling: about **56–58%** last weekend, against **69%** at the same time last year.

When prices are falling and homes sit on the market, buyers have time and room to bargain.

## 2. Interest rates are still going up

- Rates have gone up **four times this year**, to **4.60%**.
- Living costs are rising faster than expected (inflation is **4.3%** over the year). Another rate rise is possible on **3 November**. *[Final version: the 3 November decision goes here.]*

Higher rates usually push house prices down further.

## 3. The banks expect more falls until about the middle of 2027

- NAB and CommBank both expect Melbourne prices to keep falling until around **mid-2027**.
- That would be about **4–5% more** than today: roughly **$60,000–$78,000 on a $1.5 million home**.
- These are forecasts, not promises. They can be wrong in either direction.

## 4. Beachmere is a different story

- Brisbane prices, which include Moreton Bay, have **also started falling**: **4.9% down in the last three months**.
- So **holding on to Beachmere** while we wait may not help us. Waiting to **buy** in Melbourne and waiting to **sell** Beachmere are two separate decisions. We should talk about both.

## 5. A wrong choice is expensive

- **Stamp duty** on a $1.5 million home in Victoria is about **$82,500**. It's money we don't get back if we buy the wrong house and move again.
- At an **auction**, there is **no cooling-off period**. Once the hammer falls, the house is ours.

## 6. We're not ready yet

- We haven't finished the survey, agreed on the areas, or inspected homes in them.
- Some of the hills areas we liked turned out to have **bushfire rules on most streets**. We only found that out this week.

## Your trip is good timing

- You're away from **mid-November to mid-February**. That's a natural pause: no need to decide anything before you go.
- While you're away, Nish can watch prices and look at areas. When you're back, we inspect homes together from **mid-February**.
- If the banks are right, prices will be near their lowest around **April to June 2027**. That's when we'd be ready to buy.
- Selling Beachmere is its own decision. We can talk about listing it around **February**, after the summer holidays.

## What would tell us it's time to buy

1. Interest rates **stop going up**, or start coming down.
2. Prices in the suburbs we like **stop falling for two or three months**.
3. **More than 6 in 10 auctions** sell again.
4. The **right house** comes up at a price that matches recent sales nearby.
