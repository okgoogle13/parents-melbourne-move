## Bottom line

- **Budget:** The guide starts $50,000 above the parents' $1.5m cap and tops out $150,000 above it. Glen Waverley's 4-bed house median is $1,603,000, down 5.7% in a year (realestate.com.au, 12 months to Sep 2026; Checked).
- **Market timing:** Evidence suggests Melbourne house prices will keep weakening into the first half of 2027, with a likely trough around mid-2027 (buying summary base case, following NAB's October forecast; Estimate).
- **Suburb fit:** 52.3% of Glen Waverley families are couples with children (Victoria 45.5%; ABS 2021, Checked), so expect family buyers at auction (Estimate), though the house is outside the Glen Waverley Secondary zone. The house suits a family; would a four-bedroom house with lawns and established gardens to maintain suit how the parents want to live for the next 10 years?
- **Location:** Its clear strength: the family is already in Glen Waverley.
- **Nature fit:** Jells Park is nearby (listing claim). Wheelers Hill shares it at a lower median, and Endeavour Hills, Frankston and Seaford offer different and sometimes more extensive nature access at lower prices. (Not a scored comparison: Glen Waverley has no workbook nature score.)
- **Community fit (secondary priority):** Glen Waverley is already in the workbook's High community band (ABS 2021: 8.0% India-born; Checked). Of the alternatives, only Clayton South has a higher India-born share (14.9%).
- **Recommendation:** Do not bid at or above the guide unless independent comparable sales justify it and the family deliberately raises the cap.

## Why caution is warranted

The guide is above the cap before any auction competition. The buying summary's base case puts a $1.5m house at about $1.41m–$1.44m at the mid-2027 trough, a further fall of about 4–6% (Estimate). The same fall on a $1.6m purchase is about $64,000–$96,000 (Estimate), against $60,000–$90,000 on a $1.5m one. The summary does not rule out buying a suitable, well-priced house before the trough; this one is not yet shown to be well priced, and the agent's price statement (2 Oct 2026) lists no comparable sales.

## Decision test: proceed only if all are true

1. Independent settled comparable sales support a written walk-away price.
2. The family deliberately approves raising the $1.5m cap.
3. The house is genuinely better than realistic alternatives on the shortlist.
4. A building and pest inspection shows the renovation is more than cosmetic.
5. Met: Mount View Primary and Brentwood Secondary zones, not Glen Waverley Secondary's (Find My School 2027).
6. Title, planning overlays, easements, the vendor statement and land size (581 or 677 m²) raise no material issues.
7. The parents are comfortable holding the property for at least 7–10 years.

## Recommended position

**Do not bid simply because the house is appealing.** Treat it as a high-discipline exception, not a default purchase. Set a hard walk-away price before auction day from recent settled comparable sales. If the house does not clearly beat realistic alternatives on price, nature access and long-term fit, keep searching: Wheelers Hill, Endeavour Hills, Frankston, Seaford and Springvale.
