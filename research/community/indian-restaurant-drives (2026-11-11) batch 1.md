## Problems

- **Depart time.** The browser clock runs in UTC. Typing "10:00" into the Maps picker would have meant 21:00 Melbourne time. I set the departure through the URL epoch (`8j1794870000` = Tue 17 Nov 2026 10:00 AEDT) instead, so Maps displays "11:00pm Mon 16 Nov". The Advisor gate check on "Depart is Tue 17 Nov 10:00" needs this in mind.
- **Unsure places (Newtown).**
  - Desi Global Bites is mostly North Indian but has some Indo-Chinese dishes. Its Maps category is only "Restaurant".
  - Aroma Cafe is mostly South Indian but also serves mandi and cafe items.
  - Hyderabad House is the nearest unambiguous Indian restaurant, at 6 min and 2.9 km.
- **Ties.** Frankston rank 2 is a tie between Anshumann Da Dhaba and Kasturi (both 4 min, 1.8 km). Eltham's two restaurants are also tied (2 min, 0.65 km each).
- **Pin snapping.** Maps snaps each start pin to a nearby business or address, so the start is not the exact suburb centre. The coordinates in the notes are the true pin.
- **Mordialloc search.** The search "Indian restaurant near Mordialloc VIC" returned one place instead of a list, so I used a search centred on the pin.
- **Warragul and Eltham lists.** Maps returned only 2 Indian results each, and both were used. Other lists were capped at about 10 results, so a nearer place that Maps did not list could have been missed.
- **Routes.** Distance and time are for the first route Maps offers. Alternatives are noted only where they were shorter. Maps often shows one number ("typically 3 min"), so I used it for both ends of the range.
- **Method deviation.** Maps wrote unit-style addresses like "4/55 Smith St" into the URL as extra stops, which hid the depart-at setting. I redid Warragul (both), Upper Ferntree Gully (Shiraaz) and Mordialloc (Saffron) without the unit number, and confirmed "Depart at" showed on those pages.
- **Skipped sites.** No sign-in prompts or CAPTCHAs appeared.
- **Not counted.** Places permanently or temporarily closed (Royal Punjab and Staple of India, Ringwood) were excluded. Pan-Asian or non-Indian places were not counted (CurryWings, Sea Spice Kitchen). Sri Lankan Ceylon Girl's (Frankston) and Cafe Cinnamon (Mordialloc) were not routed.
