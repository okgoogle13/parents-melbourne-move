# Community comparators — research proposal (28 Oct 2026)

Status: **PROPOSAL — not in the workbook.** See the "Verification pass" section at the end for the checked figures, which supersede the estimates in the first two tables. Nothing is added to SuburbScorecard until the family approves the list (prompt section 3c).
Purpose: show the nature-versus-community trade-off side by side. Comparators are not meant to win the main rank, and by design they won't: Nature, Beach and Village carry about 44% of the weight. **No overall winner is declared.**
The ABS shares describe who lives in a suburb. They do not measure belonging or community quality.

**Interim Community band (display-only, family to confirm):**
- **High:** other language at home (AU) ≥ 50%, or India-born (AQ) ≥ 8%.
- **Medium:** AU 30–49%, or AQ 4–7.9%.
- **Low:** everything else.
- B4 drive minutes will be added later.

**Proposed set:**
- 8 comparators: 5 "Diverse / lower-green" and 3 "Potential both".
- The top 3 passing suburbs after C3 as "Green / lower-diversity" anchors.
- Rowville as the Reference row.

**Dropped:**
- Mount Waverley: fails the $1.5M purchase cap. OnTheHouse median value for houses is $1,605,243 as at 30 Jun 2026 ([suburb profile](https://www.onthehouse.com.au/suburb/vic/mount-waverley-3149)), even though its nature evidence is good (Valley, Damper Creek and Federal reserves have "significant areas of remnant native bushland" [Wikipedia]).

**Not shortlisted, kept as alternates:**
- Clayton: AQ 16.4%. OnTheHouse median value for houses $1,157,912, 31 Jul 2026 (search text). Indian grocer Sarawan Spices, 335 Clayton Rd.
- Noble Park: AQ 9.8%. Median house price $820,000 (Your Property Guide). Nearest Indian grocers found are in Dandenong South.
- Wantirna South: AQ 4.7%. House median about $1.26–1.29M (Domain/CoreLogic via agents). No nature or community place sourced.

**Drive times:**
