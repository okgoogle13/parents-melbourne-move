# Talking with Mum: how far from Glen Waverley?

Written 27 Oct 2026. Figures from the scoring workbook (SuburbScorecard, Google Maps midday drive times).

## Why this conversation
Mum's answers pull two ways:
- She picked "leafy suburb within 35 min of family" (Q13b) and changed Q10 to the street 15 min from Glen Waverley.
- She also picked a coastal or bay town (Q13c) and a beach 10 min away (Q11).
- Her note on Coorabell: it was "too remote and cut off".

The family filter allows up to 90 min. If Mum's real limit is 35 min, 23 of the 51 passing suburbs drop out.

## Show her three real places, not a number
Ask which one she would visit first, and why.

| Place | Drive to GW | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Mordialloc | 28 min | Bayside, beach and creek, train line, shops (ranked 1st) |
| Belgrave | 21 min | Forest and hills, train line terminus, village (ranked 10th) |
| Mount Martha | 47 min | Bay beaches and hills, no train, quieter (ranked 19th) |

## Four questions
1. What made Coorabell feel cut off: the distance, having no train or bus, or not knowing neighbours?
2. How often would you want to see the family in Glen Waverley: weekly, twice a week, more?
3. For that, what is the longest drive you'd be happy with: 25, 35, 45 or 60 minutes?
4. Is a 15-minute drive to the doctor and shops fine, or do you want to walk? (She rated shops and doctor Essential but chose the quiet street with a 15-min drive.)

## What each answer changes
- **Under 35 min:** the shortlist is mostly bayside and outer-east suburbs. 9 of the current top 10 fit (Mornington, at 40 min, does not).
- **Up to 45–50 min:** the Mornington Peninsula (Mornington, Dromana, Mount Martha, Rosebud, McCrae) stays in.
- **Up to 60+ min:** Macedon Ranges, Warragul and Rye stay in.
- **"Cut off" means no train:** weight train/bus higher, or make a station a filter.
- **"Cut off" means no people:** village character and community matter more than distance.
