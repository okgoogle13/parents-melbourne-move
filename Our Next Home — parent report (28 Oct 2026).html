Our Next Home: where we have got to (28 Oct 2026)

What this is. A summary of the suburbs we have looked at, scored against what the three of us said matters most. It is a research summary, not a decision.

How to read it. Every place must meet our hard limits on price, drive time to Glen Waverley and drive time to a hospital emergency department. “Provisional pass” means a place meets them on current evidence; no place is a “Confirmed pass” yet, because we have not checked any specific home. Each fact is labelled Checked (we confirmed it from a named source), Estimate (best available figure, not confirmed) or Needs verification (not yet confirmed). Scores run from 0 to 10, where 10 is the best fit for that priority.

No place in this report is described as suitable until both its hard limits and a specific home have been checked.
